title=Auto Zoom Tweaks
description=Java mod (ZombieBuddy) for Build 42: every player gets their own auto zoom, with a zoom level for each situation. Made for splitscreen (couch co-op), works in single player too.
description=
description=[h2]What's wrong in vanilla[/h2]
description=Auto zoom is one tickbox per player and nothing else. It only knows two levels, the closest and the farthest of the "Zoom levels" list, shared by all players, so you can't have auto zoom and a comfortable manual zoom list at once. In a car it always jumps to the farthest level, even standing still.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]A zoom level per situation.[/b] Indoors, melee (a zombie close by is after you), outdoors and in a vehicle, any level from 25% to 250%. Situations are detected the same way as in vanilla.
description=[*][b]Per player.[/b] Each splitscreen player (1-4) has their own levels and their own on/off.
description=[*][b]Vehicle zoom follows speed.[/b] Standing still you see as much as on foot outdoors; the view widens with speed up to the vehicle level at 80 km/h. Smooth, no jumps.
description=[*][b]Simpler options.[/b] The confusing "Zoom levels" tickboxes are gone: the table starts with the levels you had set there, and manual zoom (mouse wheel, Back wheel) keeps using them.
description=[*]Camera pan (right mouse button / RB while aiming) still zooms out, as in vanilla.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=[b]Options -> User interface -> Camera, "Auto-zoom"[/b]: the vanilla row of per-player tickboxes becomes a table: On, Indoors, Melee, Outdoors, Vehicle for every player. Works with a mouse and a controller. Changes apply at once, in game too.
description=Advanced: ~/Zomboid/AutoZoomTweaks.properties (same levels and the vehicle speed, re-read while the game runs).
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42.21+
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] 2.3.4+ installed as a Java agent. On first launch ZombieBuddy asks to approve the mod's jar. Without it the vanilla auto zoom row stays.
description=[/list]
